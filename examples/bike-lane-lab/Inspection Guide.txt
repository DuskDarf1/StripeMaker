BIKE LANE INSPECTION LIBRARY — 47 EDITABLE EXAMPLES

IMPORT
Refresh StripeMaker, open Sheets > Import Project JSON, and select Bike Lane Inspection Library.json. Leave Use imported app settings unchecked. These examples append to your existing sheets. Each sheet has a title and instructions on its background. All road geometry and markings are editable. Zoom into the crossing to inspect individual stripes.

WHAT MAKES THE SITUATIONS ACTIVATE
1. A Situation choice does not construct roads or connect bike lanes. The actual lane-to-lane links, receiving lane count, and tapers determine the crossing.
2. Lane counts INCLUDE the bike lane. A 3-to-2 vehicle-lane drop here has FOUR total lanes upstream and THREE downstream.
3. In the drop examples, upstream bike lane 4 connects to downstream bike lane 3. Upstream travel lane 3 feeds the ramp. Do not connect that travel lane to the receiving bike lane.
4. In the entry examples, upstream bike lane 3 connects to downstream bike lane 4. The ramp feeds receiving travel lane 3.
5. Use Add Outside Turn Lane in Bike Lane Setup, or right-click the right + triangle > Add Turn Lane on Right of Bike Lane. An ordinary + click adds a travel lane INSIDE the bike lane instead.
6. Join END to START nodes in the direction of traffic. Road edges merely touching on the screen are not a connection. Cases 35/36 deliberately demonstrate this.
7. An edge-to-edge branch is different from a lane-center feed. Cases 19/20 use edge links. Cases 15–18 use travel-lane feeds.
8. Keep enough segment length for the treatment: these drop examples use 100 ft before the joint; entering lane-add examples use 200 ft after it. Cases 37/38 deliberately provide only 70 ft before the drop.
9. No automatic green is used. Cases 29–32 contain explicit manual surfaces. The other cases contain none.
10. Use Download > JSON sheets: Selected sheet > Export Project JSON to send a changed case back for diagnosis. Include its case number and what differs from your intended result.

SCOPE AND VERIFICATION
This is a reproducible library of the current app’s modeled bike-lane families and useful edge cases, not every conceivable road geometry. The curbed-turn setting is exercised by crossing ramp geometry; the fixtures do not add a physical raised island. Intersection examples demonstrate approach striping only, with no assigned movements or automatic bike path through the intersection. Two-way tracks are intentionally excluded from ordinary keyhole conflict rules.
All 47 final sheets were imported into a clean browser and selected/rendered again. Editable model data matched after re-import. Expected conflict families activated; baseline and unjoined cases did not. Computed transition edges matched at connected ends, no NaN geometry occurred, and repeated cleanup did not change the geometry. Representative sheets were visually inspected. These checks establish reproducibility, not that every rendered detail is a standards-approved design.
