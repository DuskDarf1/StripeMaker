      How to Get Sheets

      Sheets are aerial photos (often called orthoimagery) of the road you're redesigning. Most states and counties publish free, high-resolution photos.

      1. Find your county or state imagery. Search for "[your county] GIS aerial imagery download" or "[your state] orthoimagery download". Pick the newest year with 3–6 inch resolution.
      2. Download your road as a GeoTIFF (.tif). GeoTIFFs carry their own scale, so StripeMaker measures them automatically.
      3. Upload it with Upload Sheet. Add as many sheets as you need and drag them into order.

      If you get stuck

      • No local imagery? The USGS National Map Downloader covers the whole U.S. at lower resolution. Choose Imagery, find your road, and download its tiles.
      • Got a .sid, .ecw, or .jp2 file? Open it in QGIS (free), right-click the layer, choose Export › Save As, and pick GeoTIFF.
      • Only have a screenshot? JPEG and PNG work too. Use Set Scale to measure one lane so markings come out the right size.
