# Lateral pavement marking sources

Markings across the lane (the Markings panel's Lateral tab) follow FDOT first, and the MUTCD where FDOT has no detail.

## Outlines (`symbols.js`)

Generated by `scripts/import-markings.py` from vector drawings. Coordinates are feet: x right of the lane's center, y forward from the marking's back edge.

- [FDOT Standard Plans Index 711-001, FY 2025–26](https://fdotwww.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/design/standardplans/2026/idx/711-001.pdf) (retrieved October 30, 2026).
  - **Sheet 1, Pavement Message and Arrow Details:** through, turn, turn-and-through, U-turn, roundabout approach, and wrong-way arrows, the preferential lane symbol, and the In Pavement Warning Marking's pedestrian figure.
    - Each detail is scaled by its own 4" × 4" grid (note 3).
    - The importer checks the scale against the dimensions, within 1" (note 2): 9'-6" through, 8' turn, 10' U-turn, 12'-9" turn and through, 13' preferential.
    - The pedestrian figure is scaled by its dimensioned 6'-0" body.
  - **Sheet 2, Markings for Merge:** the merge arrow (scaled by its grid).
  - **Sheet 13, Railroad Crossing Pavement Message:** RXR (scaled to the 20'-0" × 8'-0" X).
- **Solid arrows:** FDOT draws curved arrows with a 5–6" stencil break between stem and head. The importer bridges that break with the quadrilateral between its four facing corners, so painted arrows are solid and the outline is otherwise unchanged. Dimension text and leader arrowheads (all under 0.8 ft) are dropped.
- **Composed arrows:** left-and-right and left-right-through arrows. Neither FDOT, the MUTCD, nor the [SHS Pavement Markings chapter](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/SHSe/Pavement.pdf) gives a dimensioned version, so the importer builds them from sheet 1 parts:
  - Left-right-through: the Turn and Through arrow mirrored about its through stem.
  - Left-and-right: the Turn arrow mirrored about its own stem.
  - At about 11.8 ft wide, they scale down uniformly to fit a narrower lane (MUTCD §3B.20 ¶09, "proportionally scaled to fit within the width of the facility").
- **Roundabout dot:** the dot from sheet 1's Roundabout Approach Arrow, stored relative to that arrow's stem. It is an option on any stemmed arrow and stays on the left, the central island's side.
- [MUTCD 11th Edition (December 2023), Part 3](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part3.pdf), **Figure 3B-18**, Examples of Elongated Route Shields and Markers Applied as Pavement Markings.
  - Provides the Interstate shield and the filled U.S. route shield on its black panel (C). The figure's outline-only versions (B, D) and its generic State marker (E) are imported but not offered.
  - Sample numerals and the figure's gray pavement are removed.
  - The figure's shields are 6 ft × 15 ft, as dimensioned in MUTCD 2009 Figure 3B-25.
- **FDOT route markers:** Florida Route Marker FTP-612-25 (2-, 3-, and 4-digit faces), Toll Route Marker FTP-611-25, and Florida's Turnpike FTP-610-25.
  - The faces come from FDOT Standard Plans Index 700-102 via `assets/signs` (see `assets/signs/SOURCES.md`).
  - They are stretched lengthwise for pavement, as MUTCD §3B.22 ¶03 allows ("elongated for proper proportioning when viewed as a marking"). Neither FDOT nor the MUTCD publishes a pavement detail for them.
  - Sign strokes are converted to filled bands, so the stretch keeps them as paint.
  - Route numbers are set in the pavement lettering, filling the stretched legend area. The Turnpike's FLORIDA'S TURNPIKE legend is the face's own lettering, stretched.

The FHWA drawings are United States government works. FDOT Standard Plans are public documents of the State of Florida. Their use here does not imply endorsement.

## Built from dimensions (`js/marking-catalog.js`)

- **FDOT 711-001 sheet 2:**
  - 24" stop line.
  - Yield line: five 18" × 27" triangles per lane, equally spaced, pointing at approaching traffic.
- **FDOT 711-001 sheet 1:**
  - In Pavement Warning Marking: a 6' × 15' diamond with a 3" black border and a yellow field. The figure is centered across it, with its feet 4'-8" above the bottom point.
  - Route shields: 8' × 20' with 10'-8" numerals (limited access), or 6' × 15' with 8'-0" numerals (arterials and collectors). Note 5 widens three-digit shields; MUTCD Figure 3B-18 C's 7 ft three-digit shield gives the ratio. A square FDOT marker takes the shield width; other FDOT faces keep their proportions.
  - Note 1: pavement messages go 25' back from the stop line. Placement snaps to 25 ft back from a lane's end.
- **FDOT 711-001 sheet 8:**
  - Two-way left turn arrows: two opposing turn arrows, 8'–12'–8'.
  - Repeats default to the 300' maximum interval.
- **FDOT 711-001 sheet 10:** turn lane arrows repeat every 100' by default ("add one arrow for each 100' additional length").
- **FDOT 711-001 sheet 12:** SCHOOL between 24" white lines, 8'-0" clear on each side.
- **MUTCD 11th Edition Figure 3B-20, Yield Ahead Triangle Symbols:**
  - 45 mph or greater: 6 ft × 20 ft with a 36" base.
  - Less than 45 mph: 6 ft × 13 ft with a 30" base.
  - Both have 8" sides.
- **MUTCD 11th Edition word messages:**
  - §3B.20 ¶06–07: at most three lines, spaced 4 to 10 times the character height (32–80 ft for 8 ft letters).
  - §3B.21 ¶02: the first line is nearest the road user.
- **FDOT Design Manual 230.3.1.3 (January 1, 2026), black contrast ("High contrast"):**
  - Stop lines: a 4" border on both sides.
  - Yield lines: a block reaching 1.5" past each triangle.
  - Arrows, messages, and symbols at 45 mph or less: a 1.5" border around the outline.
  - Over 45 mph: a block reaching 1.5" past the marking.

- **FDOT 711-001 sheet 9, crosswalks:**
  - Two 12" white transverse lines.
  - Special emphasis adds 24" bars centered on every lane line and lane center (½ W), and on a two-way road's centerline. Bars stay parallel to the lane lines when the crosswalk is skewed (note 2).
  - No bar goes on an edge line: in the Special Emphasis Crosswalk Details the first bar below the edge line is on the first lane's center (checked against the FY 2025–26 sheet, October 30, 2026).
  - Width is measured inside the transverse lines: at least 6 ft at intersections and 10 ft midblock (note 1). The default is 10 ft.
  - Contrast: FDM 230.3.1.3 gives special emphasis bars a 4" black border on both sides. It names no treatment for 12" transverse lines, so they get the stop line's 4" on both sides.

## Bicycle markings

- [FDOT Standard Plans Index 711-002, Bicycle Markings, FY 2025–26](https://fdotwww.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/design/standardplans/2026/idx/711-002.pdf) (retrieved October 30, 2026):
  - **Sheet 1:** Helmeted Bicyclist Symbol (6'-0" × 3'-4"), Bike Lane Arrow (6'-0" × 2'-0"), and Shared Lane Marking (9'-4" × 3'-4"), all white. Outlines are the sheet's filled shapes, scaled from the arrow's dimensioned 6'-0" length (`scripts/import-bike-markings.py`, which checks the 2'-0" width).
  - **Sheet 2:** the symbol, 6', then the arrow; Standard Buffered Bike Lane Striping, two 6" white solid lines 8" apart.
- [FDOT Design Manual 223, Bicycle Facilities (January 1, 2027)](https://fdotwww.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/roadway/fdm/2027/2027fdm223bikes.pdf): bike lane widths (223.2.2.1), symbol and arrow placement and 1,320 ft maximum spacing (223.5.1), sharrows centered in the travel lane (223.2.6).
- FDOT 711-001 sheet 3: a 6" white line between the travel lane and the bike lane, none at the bike lane's outer edge; sheet 2: one yield triangle centered in a bike lane.
- Rebuild `assets/markings/bike.js` with `python3 -I scripts/import-bike-markings.py 711-002.pdf` (needs poppler's `pdftocairo`).

## Colored pavement and bikeway center lines

- Green bike lanes: [FDM 223.5.3](https://fdotwww.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/roadway/fdm/2027/2027fdm223bikes.pdf) (solid; match the 2'-4' dotted extension through a conflict area; 50' solid before and after).
- Red transit lanes: no FDOT standard was found, so MUTCD 11th Edition Section 3H.07 (full lane width; a broken pattern only where general traffic may enter). The broken pattern here matches the 2'-4' dotted line.
- Two-way bike track center line: FDOT gives none; MUTCD 11th Edition Part 9's yellow broken center line, drawn 6" with 3' lines and 9' gaps.
- Keyhole crossings: 711-001 sheets 5–6 (2'-4' dotted where traffic crosses), FDM 223.5.2 (150') and 223.3.2 (keyhole lanes).

## CFX markings

- [CFX 2026 Sign & Pavement Marking Design Details](https://www.cfxway.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-CFX-SPM-Design-Details.pdf) (Central Florida Expressway Authority, retrieved October 30, 2026):
  - **Sheet 47, CFX toll route shield (preformed):** 8'-0" × 20'-0". Outside edge: straight sides with 9"R top corners over a half-ellipse tip (Detail A: primary radius 4', secondary 7'-9"). 2" white outside edge, 4" black border, 4'-0" yellow TOLL panel with 33" × 37.5" black TOLL (Detail B), 4" black bar, white field with a 4" orange Florida outline, and 6'-3" black numerals (48" wide for three digits). Built from these dimensions in `js/marking-catalog.js`. The Florida outline and numeral position come from the CFX TM face (sheets 5–6, `assets/signs/CFX-TM--standard.svg`) placed in the marking's field; inset borders use inset ellipses. TOLL and numerals use the pavement lettering, fitted to the dimensioned widths.
  - **Sheet 48, colored Yield (preformed):** 6'-0" × 13'-0", white border, red background, white field, red YIELD. Drawn from the FHWA SHS R1-2 (36") face stretched to that size, point toward approaching traffic.
  - **Sheet 48, colored Interstate shield:** 6'-0" × 15'-0", white border, red top, blue field, white numeral: the same as the existing Interstate route shield marking at its 6 × 15 ft size, so it isn't a separate entry.
  - **Sheet 46, option lane directional arrow (asphalt):** 105' overall in 8" lines: 15' of line to the point of diverge, then the mainline branch to Arrow Detail A (25' × 3.5') on the lane centerline and the ramp branch to Arrow Detail B (25' × 3.5'), its tip 20' short of A's. The ramp tip's 8' offset is scaled from the drawing (not dimensioned). The concrete-surface version (larger heads, 11" contrast) isn't drawn yet; High contrast adds the usual 1.5" border.
  - Sheets 40–45 lay out standard markings, RPMs, and message sets; they add no new symbols.
- Rebuild `assets/markings/cfx.js` with `python3 -I scripts/import-cfx-markings.py` (no dependencies).
- CFX standards are agency documents; their use here does not imply endorsement.

## Longitudinal lines (`js/line-styles.js`)

- **Patterns, FDOT 711-001 sheet 2 "Pavement Marking Lines":**
  - 6" solid edge or lane line.
  - 8", 12", or 18" solid channelizing line.
  - 10'–30' skip line.
  - Dotted lines: 2'–4' guide (6"), 2'–2' extension (12"), 6'–10' extension (6"), 3'–9' interchange (6"), 3'–9' lane drop (12").
  - Double solid lines, 6"–4"–6".
  - Two-lane passing prohibited lines (solid and skip). MUTCD §3B.05 uses the same pair for two-way left-turn lanes.
- **From the MUTCD:** double skip lines for reversible lanes (MUTCD 11th Edition §3B.04), at FDOT's 6"–4"–6" and 10'–30'.
- **Contrast, sheet 2:**
  - Solid lane line: 1.5" black on both sides.
  - Skip line: 10' white, 10' black, 20' gap.
  - Dotted lines: alternating contrast. Every other dot is followed by a black block of the same length (3' white, 3' black, 6' gap, 3' white, 9' gap for 3'–9').
  - Contrast is for white lines only, and never on edge lines (FDM 230.3.1.1–230.3.1.2).
- **Dash phase:** measured along chains of joined lines, so any skip or dotted pattern continues in step through road joints and along intersection movement edges.

## Word lettering (`font.js`)

Glyph outlines come from `fonts/FHWA-Pavement-Derived.ttf`, supplied by the project owner. The font's own description reads: "Derived from public FDOT and FHWA pavement marking vector diagrams. Independently assembled; not an agency-issued font." It covers A–Z, 0–9, and space. Its cap height is the 8'-0" letter height (711-001 sheet 1). TrueType curves are flattened to M/L/Z outlines, so words export to PDF as vectors.

## Rebuild

```
python -m venv /tmp/markings-venv && /tmp/markings-venv/bin/pip install fonttools shapely
/tmp/markings-venv/bin/python -I scripts/import-markings.py 711-001.pdf part3.pdf fonts/FHWA-Pavement-Derived.ttf
```

Poppler's `pdftocairo` must be on PATH. No Python package is used by the web app.
